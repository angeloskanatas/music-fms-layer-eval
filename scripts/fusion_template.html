Layer selection

For each model and task, the best single layer (in bold) is compared with the middle and last layers, with the layers ranked highest by a label-free metric, averaged or concatenated, and with fusion of all layers, without or with training. Small numbers give the difference from the best single layer.

Held-out evaluation: when each task family's metric is selected on the other models of the same paradigm, the best layer of the held-out model is among the metric's top three in 39.6% of cases (chance: 15.1%; top one: 16.7% against 5.0%), and in 41.7% of cases for tonal tasks, where the selected metric is PTE.

Tagging scores are the mean of macro-averaged AP and AUROC.
