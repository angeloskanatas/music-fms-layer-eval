Rank = average of the model’s per-task ranks over the primary tasks (1 = best)  ·  p ranked on fewer tasks (see cells)  ·  Params = evaluated encoder, as reported (audio tower for two-tower; decoder for AR)  ·  + beyond the paper’s 12 models  ·  † metric caveat (hover)  ·  — not evaluated  ·  curve = full layer profile, dot = best layer
All numbers are our own evaluations under one fixed protocol (frozen encoder, MARBLE constrained-track MLP probe, identical splits) — they can differ from the numbers in each model’s own paper, which may use different probes, variants, or protocols (e.g. the Myna paper’s key result is for Myna-Vertical, a variant not evaluated here). Columns show each family's primary metric per dataset (AP for tagging, MIREX for chords); companion metrics (AUROC, chord root/thirds, arousal/valence …) sit behind the family expanders. Rank is the mean of per-task ranks over the primary tasks a model was evaluated on; p marks models ranked on a subset (see per-task cells). Value-means across tasks are deliberately not shown: the metrics live on different scales. Derived values are computed at build time from the raw per-layer records in data/results/. Current numbers are single-seed probes (seed 1234); missing cells are filled append-only as they are evaluated (coverage), and newly added cells run three seeds — multi-seed cells will show mean ± sd.
