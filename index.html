Each layer of the frozen encoder is evaluated separately with a trained probe: an MLP with one hidden layer of 512 units and, for most models, a linear probe (we will extend this to further probe architectures, such as attentive probes). Scores may differ from those reported in the original papers, which use other probes, checkpoints or splits. Per-layer results are available in the data folder.
